    About this journey

    Paris → Sofia. 24 September to 28 November 2019.

    Recorded route, gently rounded for the view.

    Paler red paths reconstruct missing walking sections using mapped roads and footpaths. The exact 2019 paths are unknown.

    The paper train follows mapped railway tracks from Pforzheim to Stuttgart and from Pitomača to Koška in Croatia. Stations and the service taken are inferred from the adjacent recordings. Short joins to the walking recordings are approximate.

    The railway paths use © OpenStreetMap contributors. The train is an illustrative model, enlarged to remain visible; it stays visible over mapped tunnels with subdued colours so the journey remains easy to follow. The regional connections are supported by the 2019 Baden-Württemberg service announcement and HŽ Infrastructure’s 2019 network statement.

    The recording gap near Wörthersee is shown as an estimated walking connection. A boat crossing was suggested, but the available recordings and photographs do not confirm one. The exact route through this gap is unknown.

    Walking reconstructions use Valhalla and © OpenStreetMap contributors.

    Auto follows the whole route continuously in about 15 minutes, moving quickly through open country and slowing for towns, mountains and landmarks. Choose a slower pace or a specific date to explore in your own time.

    The camera follows the direction of travel above the landscape. Photographs belong to a day; their exact position is not known. Terrain is shown at its real height.

    The landscape is drawn as paper. Trees illustrate mapped woodland; buildings follow mapped footprints with simplified heights and roofs. The map shows today’s geographic context, alongside the 2019 route and photographs. Named landmarks are deliberately enlarged paper interpretations at mapped locations, so their towers and silhouettes stand out along the journey. Their scale and details are illustrative. Their appearance along the route does not imply a visit inside.

    Distance combines the original day totals with the lengths of reconstructed walking paths. Their estimated ascent sums uphill changes in the mapped terrain profile, sampled about every 200 metres. Within-day counters and the division of unrecorded stretches between days are approximate. Train connections add neither distance nor ascent.

    Each coloured segment of the elevation timeline is one day, with its colour showing the country. Drag across the profile to move through the walk. Heights are approximate mapped ground elevation from Mapzen, sampled about every 200 metres, rather than recorded GPS altitude. Dashed portions show mapped ground beneath reconstructed paths and train connections. Mapped ground height is separate from accumulated ascent; only uphill changes along estimated walking paths contribute to the estimated climb.
