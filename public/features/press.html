a daily puzzle, out since monday 26 october 2026

features is eight small networks to untangle each day, the same for everyone, with shapes to discover along the way. drag neurons until no lines cross; each untangled feature settles into a stamp, one of 100 to collect. later boards add frozen, fused, once and linked neurons. every board is graded by turns: perfect, sharp, clean or solved, and a board can run out of turns.

where the names come from

the words come loosely from the study of what happens inside neural networks. a feature is a group of neurons; a fused neuron belongs to two features until you split it. you don’t need to know any of that to play.

how it’s made

the whole game is one html file, with no framework or build step, and downloaded sets play offline. new daily sets arrive from the server when they open. every board is made and checked before it ships, with the fewest turns it can be cleared in, which is what perfect means; sharp is one turn more and clean two.
