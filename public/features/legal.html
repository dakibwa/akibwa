privacy

  • you can play without an account: your progress and downloaded puzzle sets stay on your device. the game sends your time zone to get each new set when it opens.
  • links we post on other sites, such as features.games/features/api/go/instagram, add one to that link’s count for the day as they send you to the game, so we can tell which places people come from. the count is a number and nothing else: no address, cookie or account goes into it.
  • the game also adds one to plain daily counts when you first open it, choose how to start, first see one of today's boards, first play a move, clear the first lesson, play on a given day, first finish a day, come back the day after you started, play again on a later day or on consecutive days, see the offer to unlock features, press unlock, unlock features, turn on the daily reminder, sign in to an account, press the link to rate the game on the list that sent you, open the collection, start or finish one of the day's boards, or run out of turns on one (each counted by its place in the day, 1 to 8, and on your first tracked day by how many you have ended so far), finish a day, share a result or share one for the first time. when you finish all of today's boards, it adds one to that day's count for the points your day came to (4 a board for perfect, down to 0 for failed), once a day, and reads the day's counts back to tell you how your day compares with everyone else's. a shared result's link carries that day's grades and the sharer's name on shares (an account's first name unless they change or clear it in settings, kept only on their device), and a device that opens it keeps them to show beside its own result that day. when you first open it, it also counts whether you're on a phone or a computer. your device also keeps its first tracked play date, last tracked utc play date, consecutive-day count and how many of that first day's boards you ended. it sends only an activity category and, on the first tracked day and exactly 1, 7 or 30 days later, that cohort date, with the link you first came by and, on the later days, that number of boards; these add to aggregate counts, never a player identifier or account. your device keeps the day you started, the first of our links you came by, the link in a shared result among them (or, without one, the kind of site that sent you: a social site by name, a search engine, another site or none) and which of these it has counted, so each is counted once (playing on a day, opening the collection and starting each of its boards, once that day), and the once-only ones count towards that link too. that shows us which places bring players and where new players stop (our legitimate interest). the counts are numbers and nothing else, and if your browser sends do not track or global privacy control, the game keeps and sends nothing.
  • if you make an account, we keep your email address, nickname, sign-in method (a password only as a salted hash, and a sign-in code we email you only as a hash, for ten minutes; for apple, a sealed token kept only to disconnect apple when you delete your account), saved game, results, time zone and email settings, plus a leaderboard tag if you choose one, which is shown publicly with your scores. we use these to run your account (our contract with you).
  • the daily email comes with a new account: the card where you make one says so with a switch, on unless you turn it off there. you can also turn it on by its switch in settings or by “remind me” on a finished day’s card. it is about the game you signed up for, and you can stop it at any time with that switch or the link in every email.
  • if you unlock features, we keep an order record (payment references, amount, receipt email and restore code) to deliver it, give refunds and keep the tax records the law requires. stripe, apple or google handle your payment details under their own privacy policies.
  • our server keeps your ip address for up to two hours to stop abuse (our legitimate interest).
  • if the game hits an error on your device, it sends us the error and where in the game it happened, with your browser type and nothing from your save or account. we keep the details for up to a week to fix bugs (our legitimate interest).
  • cloudflare hosts the game and its data in western europe, stripe takes payments, resend sends our emails, and google, apple or facebook sign you in if you choose them. some of them process data outside the UK, with the safeguards UK law requires. we never sell your data.
  • we use only the sign-in cookies and device storage the game needs to work, and the small record of what it has counted above, so there’s nothing to accept.
  • we keep account data until you delete your account. we keep order records as long as tax rules require, without your email once your account is gone.
  • you can ask to see, correct, delete or take a copy of your data, or to restrict or object to how we use it: email us, or delete your account yourself. you can also complain to the information commissioner’s office at ico.org.uk.
