about these counts

devices, not people · a move counts as playing, in learn too. counts miss offline play and blocked requests; another browser or cleared storage counts again; privacy signals stop the counting. days are utc

yesterday and each day: arrived is a device's first visit; played is a device's first move of its day, counted from 2 nov; finished every board counts sets, a replay too, from 3 nov; came back is a day's players less those making their first move ever, so those who had played before, from 3 nov. the average uses full days only

the first day and where players come from compare once-a-device counts in the days shown: a first visit, a first move, a first finished set and opening the game again the day after the first visit. they are not the same devices followed step by step. a place is the counted link a device arrived by, or the site that sent it

coming back groups devices by the day they were first counted playing, from 9 nov in updated browsers, existing players among them on that day. day 1, 7 and 30 mean playing exactly that many days later; a rate appears once that day is over. days running are counted the same way. by where they came from and by how far the first day went count only devices with no play known before, from 12 and 13 nov: the link a device first came by, and how many of its first day's boards it ended

through the boards counts a device's first move on each of today's boards, from 4 nov, and the boards taken six turns past perfect while still tangled: a board failed there until 12 nov, and since then a board has no limit on its turns. after a board reads full days from 13 nov: of the boards started, those cleared; of those cleared before the day's last, those whose player started the next; and of the boards taken six turns past perfect, those cleared. keeping in touch divides each count by the count beside it, in the days shown

worth a look needs full days and at least 20 in a count before it says anything, and offers a test, never a cause. no player identifiers · the last eight weeks · read again every minute
