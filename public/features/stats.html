about these counts

devices, not people · making a move counts as active, including in learn. counts can miss offline play and blocked requests. different browsers and cleared storage can count again. privacy signals stop tracking

daily activity starts on 2 nov, partly through the day. it counts once per local game day, recorded in utc. the seven-day average uses complete days only

return tracking starts on 9 nov, also partial. it counts once per utc day in updated browsers. returning includes consecutive-day play; consecutive includes seven-day streaks. the returning share uses this tracked group only

retention groups devices by first tracked play, including existing players at rollout. day 1, 7 and 30 mean playing exactly that many days later. only completed return days enter the rates; each rate uses the eligible device total. a dash means not measured yet

funnels and source shares compare milestone counts in the selected period, not joined journeys or acquisition cohorts. first finishes start on 3 nov; board moves, failures and offer counts on 4 nov; board-seen and rate counts on 9 nov. finishes include boards run out of turns

insights use complete days and at least 20 observations for a rate. they suggest an experiment, not a cause. retention is not split by source. copy a test brief, change one thing, then compare completed cohorts

eight weeks of anonymous counts · no player identifiers · refreshed every minute
