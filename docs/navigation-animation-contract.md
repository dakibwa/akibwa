# Homepage interaction and privacy contract

Akibwa is one sheet of paper. Run `npm run check:navigation` on every build and `npm run check:navigation:dom` before publication.

## The paper redesign — 24 September 2026

Dan asked for the site to be rebuilt from first principles with the sensibility of Kevin Ngo's papercraft Claude film: really simple, a few things he cares about, more dynamic than static. He then asked for the Daniel ↔ Akibwa name change to stay, rejected a panda mascot, and asked that Taste, Career and the Trek build on what existed rather than be replaced. The name flick, the Taste wall, the career timeline and the project cards below are therefore the originals, restyled.

## Public index

- `/` introduces Daniel/Akibwa and presents five things: taste, features, websites, career and trek. The Taste Library, Career timeline and project cards are the previous homepage's approved components, restyled. The Instagram and X profiles at `@dakibwa` were explicitly approved. Do not restore the rejected capability slogans or unrelated removed personal history.
- `data/taste-curation.json` is a narrow projection of previously published career roles/dates and cultural choices. Residential history, detailed life traces, health and identifying third-party material remain excluded.
- The later 5 September request restores the eight original public career statements and expands Podcasts to the recorded show library. Only per-show counts and public catalogue metadata are included. Spotify audio/video starts lasting at least 30 seconds rank the shelf; Apple episode records remain separate. Public and subscriber Making Sense feeds are grouped. Missing provider coverage is not a zero, and music-only uploads are excluded.
- `public/listening-catalogue.json` owns the approved Spotify, YouTube, Last.fm and Apple album/show aggregates. [Listening history](listening-history.md) defines exact matching and conservative overlap bounds. Never commit raw events, account IDs or private source paths. No listening time is published, and none is inferred from scrobbles or YouTube views.
- The email control is available to people using the page but the address is assembled only after activation; it must not appear in static HTML.
- The root may be indexed, but image indexing and long search snippets are restricted. It is the only sitemap entry.
- Standalone detail surfaces such as `/trek/` use `noindex`. Crawlers remain allowed in `robots.txt` so they can read those directives. Retired pages are deleted and redirect at the edge through `public/_redirects`; do not recreate stub pages for them.

## Layout and interaction

- The page is warm cream paper (`#f3ecdf`) with the film's broad diagonal bands and a fine grain, warm ink, thin blue construction lines and tally marks. Fraunces (the Trek's face, instanced to its soft cut in `public/fonts/fraunces-soft*.woff2`) sets headings; Akibwa Sans sets labels. No page scroll is required on the front page at desktop sizes.
- The bar holds the Akibwa `a` (a papercraft trace of `public/brand-logos/akibwa-a.png`) and the Instagram, X and Email controls, each with a 44px target and a distinct accessible name. In a room the bar also lists the five things.
- “I’m Daniel.” keeps the original rhythm: the first change at 3.2 seconds, then every 4.2 seconds. A green pencil rubs the name out with its eraser and writes the other in its place; Akibwa is ember. Both names reserve their width; hidden tabs pause the cycle; reduced motion keeps Daniel still. The heading's accessible name is “I’m Daniel. Online as Akibwa.”
- “Building in the age of AI.” stays beneath the name. Top right, a round window is cut in the page with a paper wheel behind it: the hatched pencil sun (and a cloud) while the heading says Daniel, the moon and stars while it says Akibwa. As each name change begins, `NameFlip` sets `<html data-sky>` and the wheel turns half a revolution about a pin below the window (1.5s, settling with a little give): through sunset bands to the moon, then on through dawn bands back to the sun. Unset means day, so loading turns nothing; reduced motion keeps Daniel and the sun.
- Text on the page is not selectable (`user-select: none` on the body, including the Trek); inputs, text areas and editable fields still select.
- Five things stand on one pencil ground line: a hand of cards drawn as cartoon sleeves of Taking Tiger Mountain (By Strategy), Person Pitch and Graceland that fans open (taste); a Features tile, five blue threads on the game's paper dealt as a five-pointed star, that untangles into a heart — the threads bend into its outline and it colours in rose — without ever becoming a stamp or a coloured plate (features); no Claude-like orange field; three fanned browser windows drawn from Butterfly Rose, Castle Bank and Português com a Inês (websites); a paper plane on a looping trail (career); and a folded map (trek). On arrival each is sketched in pencil and then lands as paper; returning shows them at rest. Hover or focus lifts a thing and draws a pencil rule under its label. On phones they become a list.
- Choosing a thing opens its room in place (a view transition carries the thing into the room's heading). Each room is one history entry carrying its hash, so Back and Escape return to the five things; a shared room link opens straight into the room with the front page behind it in history and the bar in view. Focus moves to the room heading. Without JavaScript, room links still work through `:target`. The trek opens `/trek/`.
- **taste** is the Taste Library in its full-width mode: the stacked wall, the flush detail that unfolds beside a cover with its count, search and the coloured filters. Songs (the top 1,000) and Artists (the top 100) lead the filters; Albums, Films, Games, TV and Podcasts follow. Each row of the mixed wall opens with one of the most played songs. The page carries the first 60 songs and every artist; the rest load from `public/music-ranking.json` when asked for. Cards never navigate.
- **features** is a small untangle in the game's own palette: blue threads on warm paper (`#EAE7DF`), each crossing marked by a small ink ring, dots that drag or move with the arrow keys, and “crossings left” in the game's words. Each shape is a ring of neurons dealt as a star; untangled, the dots settle onto its outline, the threads bend into it and the shape colours in — a heart first, then a house and a leaf (`components/paper/feature-shapes.mjs`, from the game's stamp artwork). Found shapes fill in beside the wordmark. A solved shape stays the thing it is; do not turn it into a stamp. The wordmark keeps its four-colour rule. It links to features.games.
- **websites** is the previous homepage's project cards, spotlit so every description prints, three to a row as paper (two on narrower screens): Português com a Inês, Castle Bank and Butterfly Rose — the three Dan chose on 24 September 2026. Castle Bank's capture hides its founders' named photographs. Butterfly Rose is unlinked (“coming soon”) while it is in review with the salon.
- **career** is the original timeline: logo cards on a pencil node line, the current role's statement resting beneath it, hover, focus or a tap moving it under another role.
- Reduced motion removes the drawing, spinning, bobbing and view transitions; everything appears at rest.

## Standalone project surfaces

- Project cards open standalone destinations: `https://portuguesewithines.com/` and `https://www.castle-bank.com/`. Do not add `from=akibwa` or activate an Akibwa portfolio header on the destination.
- The Trek is always standalone and excluded from search and image indexes. Its existing exact public route and factual-only journal remain governed by the Trek privacy checker; the [Trek contract](trek-design.md) owns presentation, geographic limits and source allow-lists.
- Trek opens as an interactive terrain relief with an SVG Atlas alternative, explicit playback controls and reachable photo/music context on phones and desktop. It uses the existing public geography and cached elevation.

## Verification — 24 September 2026

`npm run check:navigation` covers the routes, search posture, private contact, the name change's timing, the five things, the restored components, the public song ranking's fields and the Taste layout algorithm. `npm run check:navigation:dom` renders the front page at 320–1920px, rooms and history, the Taste wall with songs and artists, career, websites, a keyboard solve of the puzzle into a coloured heart, the sky turning to the moon with the name, unselectable text, rooms without JavaScript, reduced motion and a clean console.
