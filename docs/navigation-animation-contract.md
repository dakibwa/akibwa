# Homepage interaction and privacy contract

Akibwa is one sheet of paper. Run `npm run check:navigation` on every build and `npm run check:navigation:dom` before publication.

## The paper redesign — 24 September 2026

Dan asked for the site to be rebuilt from first principles with the sensibility of Kevin Ngo's papercraft Claude film: really simple, a few things he cares about, more dynamic than static. He then asked for the Daniel ↔ Akibwa name change to stay, rejected a panda mascot, and asked that Taste, Career and the Trek build on what existed rather than be replaced. The name flick, the Taste wall, the career timeline and the project cards below are therefore the originals, restyled.

## Public index

- `/` introduces Daniel/Akibwa and presents five things: music, features, websites, career and trek, with the hints “my taste archive”, “untangle a neural net”, “sites I’ve built”, “how I got here” and “my journey across Europe” (Dan, 25 September 2026). The career roles and project cards are the previous homepage's approved components, restyled. The Instagram and X profiles at `@dakibwa` were explicitly approved. Do not restore the rejected capability slogans or unrelated removed personal history.
- `data/taste-curation.json` is a narrow projection of previously published career roles/dates and cultural choices. Residential history, detailed life traces, health and identifying third-party material remain excluded.
- The later 5 September request restores the eight original public career statements and expands Podcasts to the recorded show library. Only per-show counts and public catalogue metadata are included. Spotify audio/video starts lasting at least 30 seconds rank the shelf; Apple episode records remain separate. Public and subscriber Making Sense feeds are grouped. Missing provider coverage is not a zero, and music-only uploads are excluded.
- `public/listening-catalogue.json` owns the approved Spotify, YouTube, Last.fm and Apple album/show aggregates. [Listening history](listening-history.md) defines exact matching and conservative overlap bounds. Never commit raw events, account IDs or private source paths. No listening time is published, and none is inferred from scrobbles or YouTube views.
- The email control is available to people using the page but the address is assembled only after activation; it must not appear in static HTML.
- The root may be indexed, but image indexing and long search snippets are restricted. It is the only sitemap entry.
- Standalone detail surfaces such as `/trek/` use `noindex`. Crawlers remain allowed in `robots.txt` so they can read those directives. Retired pages are deleted and redirect at the edge through `public/_redirects`; do not recreate stub pages for them.

## Layout and interaction

- The page is warm cream paper (`#f3ecdf`) with the film's broad diagonal bands and a fine grain, warm ink, thin blue construction lines and tally marks. Fraunces (the Trek's face, instanced to its soft cut in `public/fonts/fraunces-soft*.woff2`) sets headings; Akibwa Sans sets labels. No page scroll is required on the front page at desktop sizes.
- The bar carries no wordmark (Dan, 25 September 2026). On the front page the Instagram, X and Email controls sit under the lede, each with a 44px target and a distinct accessible name; in a room the bar holds a pencil arrow back to the five things, the five things by name and the same three controls. The room on show sits on an ink tab (the albums/songs switch's pill) that slides to the next room; the bar keeps still through the view transition so the slide is seen. There is no pencil underline.
- “I’m Daniel” (no full stop since 25 September 2026) keeps the original rhythm: the first change at 3.2 seconds, then every 4.2 seconds. One name wipes away and the other wipes in, with no pencil; Akibwa is ember. Both names reserve their width; hidden tabs pause the cycle; reduced motion keeps Daniel still. The heading's accessible name is “I’m Daniel. Online as Akibwa.”
- “Building in the age of AI.” stays beneath the name. Top right, a round window is cut in the page with a paper wheel behind it: a radiant sun in Urizen's deep red, after Blake — a hatched crimson disc in a soft glow with sixteen long and short rays — while the heading says Daniel, and a deep blue moon on a dusk-blue sky with pale stars while it says Akibwa (Dan, 25 September 2026). As each name change begins, `NameFlip` sets `<html data-sky>` and the wheel turns half a revolution about a pin below the window (1.5s, settling with a little give): through sunset bands to the moon, then on through dawn bands back to the sun. Unset means day, so loading turns nothing; reduced motion keeps Daniel and the sun.
- Text on the page is not selectable (`user-select: none` on the body, including the Trek); inputs, text areas and editable fields still select.
- The mascot is the Akibwa `a` in the mark's own proportions, a size up, with two square eyes centred on its arch, where they have room to look about (`components/paper/mascot.jsx`). It is never drawn in. On a first visit it starts inside the sky's round window, shrunk to fit where the sun will be (the layout's first-paint script sets `data-sky-host` so the sun never flashes), watches the five things drawn in, then leaps out to its spot at about 2.5 seconds, growing to full size, and the sun (or moon) rises into the window. Reduced motion and shared room links skip this. It dances in the open middle of the sheet (sways, bops, twirls, wags its tail, wobbles), follows the pointer with its eyes, hops when the heading writes Akibwa, and every so often hops onto one or two of the five things to inspect them; a visited thing answers exactly as a hovered one (`.thing.is-visited`). On phones it stands beside the contact links and, rather than hopping across the words, keeps to the right-hand edge and leans in from the margin beside a thing in view. It can be dragged and set down anywhere, which becomes its home; a click makes it twirl. It rests while a room is open, and reduced motion keeps it still.
- Five things stand in a row on their own soft shadows, with no ground line (Dan removed it on 25 September 2026): a hand of cards drawn as cartoon sleeves of Taking Tiger Mountain (By Strategy), Person Pitch and Graceland that fans open (music); the first Features tile — five neurons dealt as a star with a chord on an orange square, falling into the house's corners as its ink silhouette appears (features); two browser windows that fan open, the front one carrying Português com a Inês's blobs on navy (websites); a paper plane on a looping trail (career); and a folded map whose dotted route walks on towards the pin while it is hovered (trek). On arrival each is sketched in pencil and then lands as paper; returning shows them at rest. Hover or focus lifts a thing; its label is not underlined. On phones they become a plain list with no ruled lines, each drawing cropped to what is drawn so its label centres on it.
- Choosing a thing opens its room in place (a view transition carries the thing into the room's heading). Each room is one history entry carrying its hash, so Back and Escape return to the five things; a shared room link opens straight into the room with the front page behind it in history and the bar in view. Focus moves to the room heading. Without JavaScript, room links still work through `:target`. Rooms have no title on the page: each keeps a visually hidden heading for readers.
- **music** is a switch — albums or songs — over square sleeves (covers are never cropped) packed edge to edge with no holes (`components/paper/music-map.mjs`): sides are whole cells of a grid as wide as the map, following the square root of the hours, the most listened first from the top left, and the grid is filled cell by cell with a tally that makes it come out exact. No sleeve is smaller than a cell (about 32px for albums and 23px for songs on desktop); a small one grows to a readable size, square and inside the map, with its name and hours, under the pointer or a tap. Music for Psychedelic Therapy is a cell larger than Graceland and Discreet Music a cell smaller, at Dan's request; their corners keep their real hours. Songs are typeset rather than shown by a sleeve (the sleeve is the album's, not the song's): the title large and the artist beneath on a paper tint, one tint to an artist, and the tiniest show the title's first letter until they grow. All 1,000 songs show at once. There is no explanatory text. Sleeves drawn large use an 800px rung (`scripts/build-music-art.mjs`). Choosing an album opens its track list on a sheet with plays and hours per song; Escape or the close button returns to the room. The page carries every album without tracks and the hundred most listened songs; `public/music-ranking.json` loads when the room opens.
- **features** frames the Features game itself (`/features/`, the copy the Features repository publishes here) in place, filling the room under the bar like the trek, without `from=akibwa`, so the game wears no portfolio banner; it loads the first time the room opens (Dan, 25 September 2026). The front page's thing keeps the first version's orange house untangle.
- **websites** is the previous homepage's project cards, spotlit so every description prints in eight words or fewer, three to a row as paper (two on narrower screens): Português com a Inês, Castle Bank and Butterfly Rose — the three Dan chose on 24 September 2026. Each card is an illustration, not a screenshot of the site. Cards sit still on hover (only the caption tints). Butterfly Rose is unlinked (“coming soon”) while it is in review with the salon.
- **career** shows every role on its own card, the most recent first, three to a row (two, then one, as the sheet narrows): logo, name, role and years, and the statement. The cards arrive one after another when the room opens. A railway line once ran through them; Dan asked for it to go (25 September 2026).
- **trek** frames the standalone `/trek/` journey in place, filling the room under the bar, with no link out; it loads the first time the room opens. Framed, `/trek/` hides its own masthead and sends its links to the whole window.
- Reduced motion removes the drawing, spinning, bobbing and view transitions; everything appears at rest.

## Standalone project surfaces

- Project cards open standalone destinations: `https://portuguesewithines.com/` and `https://www.castle-bank.com/`. Do not add `from=akibwa` or activate an Akibwa portfolio header on the destination.
- `/trek/` stays a standalone page (the trek room frames it) and is excluded from search and image indexes. Its existing exact public route and factual-only journal remain governed by the Trek privacy checker; the [Trek contract](trek-design.md) owns presentation, geographic limits and source allow-lists.
- Trek opens as an interactive terrain relief with an SVG Atlas alternative, explicit playback controls and reachable photo/music context on phones and desktop. It uses the existing public geography and cached elevation.

## Verification — 24 September 2026

`npm run check:navigation` covers the routes, search posture, private contact, the name change's timing, the five things, the restored components, the public song ranking's fields and the music map (no holes or overlaps, the most listened first). `npm run check:navigation:dom` renders the front page at 320–1920px, rooms and history, the ink tab, the mascot leaving the sky, the square music map and switch with all the songs and a small sleeve growing, the career cards, websites, the framed trek and Features game, the sky turning to the moon with the name, unselectable text, rooms without JavaScript, reduced motion and a clean console.
